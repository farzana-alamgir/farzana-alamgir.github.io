I am an Assistant Professor of Economics in the Department of Finance, Economics and Decision Sciences at Algoma University (Brampton campus). I received my PhD in Economics from McMaster University in 2024.

My research is in international macroeconomics, with a focus on sovereign default, sovereign spreads, and the macroeconomic effects of commodity price uncertainty. My work has been published in the Journal of Economic Dynamics and Control. My current research also studies how mortgage credit expansion affects productive investment.

I teach Intermediate Macroeconomics and International Finance at the undergraduate level, and International Economics at the graduate level.
